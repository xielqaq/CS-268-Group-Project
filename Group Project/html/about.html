About SACM

SACM is a student organization at UW-Eau Claire for students who are interested in computer science and technology.

The club gives students opportunities to meet other students, participate in events, work on projects, and learn more about technology outside of the classroom.

SACM also provides a place for students to build connections, share ideas, and get involved in the computer science community.

What We Do

SACM organizes events, activities, and projects that help students learn more about computer science and technology.

Members can meet other students, share ideas, and participate in activities outside of the classroom.
